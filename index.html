HOW BASESTOCK10 WORKS

One wallet. One Glider account. Eight-stock exposure.

BaseStock10 is a non-custodial interface for a Glider-managed Base portfolio. You approve every wallet transaction; the app never holds your wallet keys.

01 · CONNECT

Connect your wallet

Connect a Base wallet. Your wallet uses one Glider account for this strategy.

02 · FUND

Invest USDC

Send USDC to the Glider Base account created for your wallet.

03 · REBALANCE

Follow the basket

After indexing, ask Glider to rebalance into the current eight-stock basket.

04 · TRACK & WITHDRAW

Stay in control

See your value and holdings, then withdraw available indexed assets when you want.
